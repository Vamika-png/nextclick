You stay in control

It guides. You click.

NextClick was built for people trying software for the first time, so it is careful by design.

✋

Never clicks or types for you

Nothing in NextClick moves your mouse or presses keys. Clicks pass straight through to your apps.

◉

Looks only when you ask

It asks permission first. Then it takes one screenshot when you press Show me or ask a question.

▢

Nothing is recorded

Screenshots are never saved to your computer. Pause it any time, for example before typing a password.

⚿

Your key stays with you

You connect your own AI key once. It’s stored encrypted on your PC and only sent to your AI provider.

Questions

Good to know.

Is NextClick available yet?

Not fully. NextClick is in early access: join the waitlist and we’ll invite you as we open up. If you’re happy to test something unfinished, the Windows early preview on this page works today.

Is NextClick free?

Yes, the app is free. It uses an AI to read your screen and plan, with your own key. Google’s Gemini has a free tier that works; if you use NextClick a lot, the free limits can slow it down, and turning on billing in Google AI Studio fixes that.

Will it click or type for me?

No. NextClick shows you where to click and what to paste. You do every click and keystroke yourself, so you learn the tool as you go.

Which apps does it work with?

Anything on your screen. It comes with a toolkit of 133 founder tools, from ChatGPT and Figma to Razorpay, HubSpot and Product Hunt, and it can help in any other app too.

Does it record my screen?

No. It takes one screenshot only when you ask, sends it to your AI provider to understand it, and never saves it. You can pause NextClick at any time, and it reminds you to pause before passwords. On Gemini’s free tier, Google may use requests to improve its products; turn on billing if that matters to you.

Why does Windows show a warning?

New apps need a code-signing certificate before Windows trusts them. NextClick doesn’t have one yet, so Windows asks you to confirm. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet. NextClick runs on Windows 10 and 11 today.
